What it does

There are not enough practitioners expertise in the field of dermatology that can diagnose the skin condition. therefore, the application is designed to help the practitioners in other province where access to expert dermatologist is limited have decision support tools.

My contribution

I built the structure for front and back of the application Flutter, Python and Flask along with the training of models using PyTorch.
