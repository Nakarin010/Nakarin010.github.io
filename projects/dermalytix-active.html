Mission Brief

Dermalytix Active is a collaborative senior project that applies machine learning and active learning to skin cancer diagnosis through a mobile application.

Objective

Explore decision support for practitioners in provinces where access to specialist dermatologists is limited.

Subject Contribution

I worked with my teammates to build the application’s frontend and backend using Flutter, Python, and Flask, and trained models with PyTorch.
